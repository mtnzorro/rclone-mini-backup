Privacy policy

rclone mini backup is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It requests the drive.file scope, so it can access only the files it creates in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Backup data is encrypted on the owner's computer before upload.
  • No data is collected, stored elsewhere, shared, or sold. There are no analytics or third parties.
